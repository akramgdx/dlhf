THE PREMISE

You see her silhouette across the chasms of the world—just beyond the next ridge, standing quietly in the fading twilight.

You keep moving. Through crumbling stones, thorns, forgotten forests, and desolate ruins. Every step is an attempt to bridge the gulf that opened between you.

"If I can just reach her, maybe things can be the way they were before."

Collect the scattered memory fragments along your journey to uncover the moments that time tried to erase.
